# Egypt leave management MVP

Shiftly HR ships a bilingual leave module for private-sector tenants using Egypt Labour Law No. 14 of 2025 as the protected statutory baseline. Company policy may be more generous, but the default catalogue must not be used to reduce a statutory entitlement.

## Included workflow

1. An employee or authorized HR user submits a dated leave request.
2. If the employee has a recorded manager, the manager reviews first. Employees without a manager move directly to owner review.
3. A company owner performs the final approval or rejection.
4. Final approval creates dated leave records and posts any balance deduction to an append-only leave ledger.
5. Every request, decision, policy change, holiday change, and balance adjustment is tenant-isolated and audited.

## Statutory defaults

- Annual leave: 15 days in year one, 21 days from year two, 30 days after ten total service years or age 50, and 45 days for persons with disabilities or dwarfism. Service below one year is prorated after six months. Qualifying hazardous, unhealthy, or remote work adds seven days.
- Annual leave excludes official holidays and weekly rest days. At least 15 days should be used annually, including at least six consecutive days. Balances or their cash equivalent are settled at least every three years.
- Emergency leave: seven days per year, no more than two days per occurrence, deducted from annual leave.
- Exam leave: actual paid exam days with proof and ten days advance notice; it is outside the annual balance.
- Child-birth day: one paid day on the date of birth, up to three occurrences during service.
- Hajj or Jerusalem: one paid month after five consecutive service years, once during service.
- Maternity: four paid months, at least 45 days after delivery, up to three occurrences.
- Unpaid childcare: up to two years, up to three occurrences, subject to the statutory service, establishment-size, and spacing conditions.
- Sick, industrial sick, and contagious-contact leave retain medical certification and pay-tier data for payroll processing.
- Public-holiday work can be compensated according to the law or by an approved substitute day request.

## Official calendar

The default tenant seed contains the 21 individual dates in Egypt's Presidency-published 2026 national holiday calendar, including the multi-day Eid periods. Administrators can add or correct future official dates when the relevant authority publishes them. Official holidays do not reduce annual leave.

## Important product boundary

This module is an operational compliance aid, not legal advice or a replacement for review by Egyptian labour counsel. Medical compensation, payroll treatment, establishment classification, and newly issued ministerial or cabinet decisions remain configurable and should be reviewed before payroll publication.
